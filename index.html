RESEARCH PROFILE

Research in metaheuristic optimization.

Publication · 2025

Stochastic Diffusion Adaptive Optimization: a novel metaheuristic approach

Ricardo Miguel Leal Lopez · Discover Analytics, Springer Nature

SDAO replaces gradient descent with a density-based diffusion mechanism derived from Fick's second law, allowing particles to escape densely populated regions and explore under-sampled areas. It incorporates global and individual guidance, adaptive parameter tuning, opposition and periodic boundary contraction. It consistently outperformed SHADEwithILS, AMSO and TLPSO in noisy and high-dimensional environments, with ANOVA, Tukey HSD and Wilcoxon statistical validation.
